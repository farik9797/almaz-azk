About the company

AZK Almaz LLP — steady growth and deep experience since 1998

For more than 25 years AZK Almaz LLP has been a key player in the petroleum products market of southern Kazakhstan. We specialise in supplying quality fuel: AI-92, AI-95, diesel — for industrial, agricultural and transport enterprises and for retail motorists.

Our own modern tank farm in Shymkent, a chemical laboratory and a fleet of special vehicles allow us to guarantee uninterrupted supply, accurate volume accounting and strict compliance with all technical standards.

Five steps to a successful shipment

A transparent, well-run process from your first enquiry to the complete set of documents.

Enquiry

A request on the site or a call to a manager

Matching terms

Paperwork

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Answers to our clients' questions

How long has the company been operating?

AZK Almaz LLP has been in the petroleum products market since 1998 — more than 25 years.

Which types of fuel do you sell?

AI-92 and AI-95 petrol and diesel fuel — all our products meet quality requirements and come with documentation.

Do you work with companies?

Yes, wholesale is one of our core activities: supply to corporate clients, industrial enterprises, agricultural organisations and public institutions, including fuel cards and vouchers.

How can a delivery be paid for?

Payments are made by any lawful method in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the terms of the signed contract.

What should I do if I have a question about fuel quality or quantity?

Contact the company — every enquiry is handled individually, with verification and analysis of the accompanying documentation.

Which regions do you serve?

Our tank farm and station network are located in Shymkent and Turkistan Region; we supply all southern regions of Kazakhstan.
