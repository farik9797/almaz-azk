About the company

AZK Almaz LLP — trusted fuel supplier in Southern Kazakhstan since 1998

For over 25 years, AZK Almaz LLP has been providing reliable fuel solutions to businesses and retail customers across Southern Kazakhstan. We supply high-quality gasoline AI-92 and AI-95, Euro IV diesel fuel to companies in the transportation, agricultural, industrial, and commercial sectors, while also serving thousands of motorists through our network of service stations.

Our own oil depot in Shymkent enables us to maintain stable inventory levels, ensure uninterrupted supply, and uphold strict quality control standards throughout the entire supply chain.

Q&A

Frequently asked questions

Answers to our clients' questions

How long has ALMAZ been in business?

ALMAZ has been a trusted participant in Kazakhstan’s petroleum products market since 1998. With over 25 years of experience, the company continues to provide reliable fuel solutions for both private and business customers.

What fuel products are available at ALMAZ stations?

ALMAZ offers AI-92 gasoline, AI-95 gasoline, and diesel fuel. All products meet established quality standards and are supported by the appropriate technical and regulatory documentation.

Do you work with businesses and corporate clients?

Yes. Wholesale fuel supply is one of ALMAZ’s core business areas. We work with corporate customers, industrial enterprises, agricultural organizations, and government institutions, providing reliable fuel supply solutions tailored to operational needs. Our services include corporate fuel cards, fuel vouchers, and flexible purchasing arrangements designed to support efficient fuel management.

How can I pay for fuel deliveries?

Payments can be made using any legally accepted payment method in accordance with the laws and regulations of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the terms of the applicable agreement.

What should I do if I have a question about fuel quality or quantity?

Please contact our customer service team directly. Every enquiry is reviewed individually and thoroughly. To ensure an accurate assessment, each case is examined together with the relevant supporting documentation, delivery records, and quality certificates where applicable. We are committed to addressing all concerns promptly, professionally, and transparently.

What regions do you serve?

ALMAZ’s oil depot and network of service stations are strategically located in Shymkent and the Turkistan Region, enabling efficient fuel distribution throughout southern Kazakhstan.

Have more questions?

Our team is here to help. Contact the ALMAZ Sales Department for personalized assistance, product information, and expert guidance on fuel supply, corporate services, fuel cards, and partnership opportunities.
